# Source policy

Priority: OEM/service information > official standards metadata > government datasets > open curated databases > community references.

Import rules:
- CC0/MIT-compatible factual data may be transformed into KARETA schema with provenance.
- ODbL datasets stay reference-only unless an explicit share-alike database decision is made.
- Copyrighted standards and OEM manuals are not copied verbatim; store citations/identifiers and independently authored summaries.
- Community repair steps never become VERIFIED solely because they exist in a repository.
- Manufacturer-specific DTCs require make/model/year/engine/ECU applicability and source traceability.
